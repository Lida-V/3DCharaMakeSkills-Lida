# Afterword: Lida's own design choices

[Japanese source](../lida-design.md) · Source baseline: 2026-10-20.

This short section records Lida's design preferences separately from general technical rules. Update it to the final choices when production is complete.

- Check rounded cheeks and the underside of the chin from the front, side, and oblique views.
- Hair is silver-white to pale lavender with a soft sheen. Avoid unnecessary branching at strand tips.
- Eyes and expressions retain the original eye design and facial character.
- Clothing emphasizes layered frills and silver ornamentation; wings emphasize the art's silhouette and feather overlap.
- Choose an appearance without excessively strong chest shading or shoulder roundness.

Fixed-jaw requirements, joint axes, topology, penetration, saving, and export checks are technical conditions in their respective stages, not optional preferences in this afterword. [Return to the process index](process-index.md).
