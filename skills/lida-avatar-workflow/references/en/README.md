# Reference documents

[Japanese source](../README.md) · English edition based on the source as of 2026-10-21.

The shared original diagrams retain Japanese labels. Read them together with the English captions and legends in these chapters, which explain the numbered regions, colors, fixed areas, and directions. The diagrams are instructional schematics unless explicitly identified as actual model evidence.

[Use the included finished model as a production reference](model-reference.md): the official finished ZIP includes China/Wafuku character r35, which stays with the skill folder when installed. Inspect face, hands/fingers, joints, sleeves, materials, secondary motion, and expressions at relevant stages. Redistribution of the model and sale of modified versions are prohibited under terms separate from those for the documents and code.

For finger dents, thumb axes, tongue and lower teeth, FBX display problems, or sleeve folds found during a new production, read [practical feedback from another character](generation-feedback.md). Diagnose the cause rather than copying example settings.

Start with a [production plan](production-plan.md). Include diagnosis and any necessary corrections for mesh structure, intersections, materials, hand and finger movement, and eyes. Record the permitted material changes and reasons for deferral so work does not fall through gaps while waiting for separate instructions.

Use [quality proposals and stage goals](quality-goals.md) to define the intended finished result and the boundary of each stage.

Find the required method in the [process index](process-index.md). Numbers and counts describe the example's conditions; candidates and schematics do not guarantee a finished model.

For a new moving humanoid, establish its neutral shape and joint topology, then use [Tripo VRM 1.0 auto-rigging with an FBX no larger than 150 MB](tripo-autorig.md). Check the received initial skeleton and preservation results before adjusting bones, topology, and weights.

If eye, mouth, or emotion references are missing, [generate expression sheets and individual images](expression-sheets.md). Plan this during initial material preparation, then connect the required individual 3D references to local keys on one face.

For accessories that shine too much, look cloudy, or lose detail, use [accessory material correction](accessory-materials.md). For missing surfaces, incorrect connections, or insufficient volume, use [mesh repair and completion](mesh-repair-completion.md). Combine the cause-selection diagrams with concrete construction, preservation, and verification steps.

Connect CLI checks of skeleton structure, file size, and preservation to [images and motion under matching conditions](mechanical-visual-review.md). Mechanical results alone do not establish visual acceptance or completion.

Choose diagrams for directions, regions, and connections from the [diagram index](diagram-index.md). Alongside each diagram, identify the corresponding model regions, numbers, fixed parts, and test motions; do not judge from the illustration alone.

To distinguish an ordinary blink from smiling closed eyes, use [wink closure](wink-closure.md). Separate eye-only deltas and left/right control, then check adjacent surfaces, shadow layers, and intermediate motion together.

When finishing materials for another destination, use the [procedure for separating color management, shading, gloss, outlines, and transparency](cross-engine-look.md). It also covers mouth interiors, feather edges, and reference images and presets across shots.
