# How to document a change

[Japanese / 日本語](CONTRIBUTING.md) · Source baseline: 2026-10-20.

Briefly describe four things for new knowledge:

- **Problem:** Location, pose, and appearance; whether it also occurs in neutral shape.
- **Effective method:** How axes/amounts were chosen, transition-back region, and preserved parts.
- **Verified scope:** Matching-condition images, intermediate motion, left/right, dependencies, saving, and destination.
- **Next request:** Consolidate conditions added during the work into one initial request.

Separate specific dimensions/counts and assignee assessments from general methods: they describe the example's conditions. Do not present a candidate image as an accepted finished example. If major defects remain, do not record a tiny line improvement as their resolution.

After additions/corrections, run the checks in [updating](UPDATING.en.md). Keep technical detail in dedicated reference chapters instead of copying the same explanation into several stages.
