This production example starts with reference images and models generated through services such as Tripo, followed by adjustments to shape, expression keys, bones/skin weights, clothing/hair/wing following, and toon materials. The finish includes iterative production work. It is neither the result of the initial generation alone nor a guarantee of automatically reproducing the same quality.

Display conditions are Blender 5.2.1 LTS / EEVEE, the production model's LidaSoftToon, gray background, Standard view transform, and exposure 0. The full-body view is oblique orthographic; the face is a front bust view. These images do not demonstrate identical appearance from merely applying the separately distributed general-purpose add-on or using Unity/UE.

For PNGs, unnecessary capture metadata/local paths were removed, then the original image was placed on a backing with 98 pixels of bottom margin containing “© 2026 Lida · 3DCharaMakeSkills-Lida” and the source URL. The original 820×1000 pixel region and color-display information were verified unchanged. Image editing did not correct face, proportions, or brightness.

Both videos preserve their full original framing (1448×900 or 1920×1080) and add an 84-pixel bottom band continuously showing “© 2026 Lida” and the source URL. Because they were re-encoded, pixel equality to unbanded renders is not claimed. They use camera orbit, original procedural motion, existing expression keys, and spring simulation, without reference dance FBX.
